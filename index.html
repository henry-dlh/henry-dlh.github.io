Projects

HeatSense Safety System

Dec 2025 – June 2026

HeatSense is a safety system that watches a stovetop with a thermal camera and a PIR motion sensor, then warns before a fire starts. I led a 4-person team on this one, splitting the build across hardware, firmware, and testing and keeping us on track with a shared Gantt chart. My own piece was the embedded pipeline — pulling frames off the thermal array through an ESP32, then combining thermal rate-of-change with PIR presence data so the classifier could tell "someone's actively cooking" apart from "the stove was left on unattended." We validated the whole system together through a live-fire test campaign before calling it done.

  • Built an embedded ESP32 pipeline streaming live thermal heatmap data to a web dashboard
  • Designed and validated the safety classifier through iterative live-fire testing
  • Designed the 3D-printed enclosure and led a 4-person team to deliver on schedule

SDR Wireless Communication System — Electromagnetic Systems

Dec 2025 – Feb 2026
  • Achieved end-to-end QPSK transmission and reception across SDRs by building a Python baseband pipeline with RRC pulse shaping, pilot-based channel equalization, and symbol detection
  • Corrected CFO between two Pluto SDRs using 4th-power estimation and cross-correlation frame sync, reducing SER by 40% and eliminating constellation rotation
  • Implemented OFDM with FFT/IFFT subcarrier mapping and cyclic prefix to mitigate ISI under multipath
  • Demonstrated matched filter SNR gain across 64-QAM and 256-QAM at varying transmit gains, confirming consistent SER improvement

Autonomous Line-Following Vehicle — Power Electronics

Sept 2024 – Nov 2024

This is an autonomous robot that follows a line on the floor and handles intersections entirely on its own, built on the MSP432 microcontroller. I designed the whole control loop myself — reading eight IR sensors to track the line, feeding that into a custom PD controller to keep the robot centered, and layering in intersection detection so it could recognize a checkpoint and execute a full spin without any human input. I also built in adaptive speed control, so it moves faster on straightaways and automatically slows through turns instead of running at one fixed speed the whole course.

  • Built an MSP432-based robot with 8 IR sensors, PWM motor control, and a custom weighted PD controller
  • Engineered majority-sensor logic to reliably detect intersections and trigger checkpoint maneuvers
  • Added adaptive speed control to speed up on straights and slow through turns
